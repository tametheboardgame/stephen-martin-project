# The Stephen Martin Project

An intentionally small adult British comedy project with a fictionalised story about Stephen Martin travelling to China. Except for the titular name **Stephen Martin**, the project does not publish real-world identity details.

## Deliverables
1. **Primary:** `Rescue Stephen Martin from China`, a **15–25 minute** humorous point-and-click/touch browser game, with three compact endings.
2. **Parallel:** One funny, polished comedy song, building on the Berserk Llama Syndrome aesthetic.
3. **Optional:** A short pilot for a digital comic, with further production only after a separate approval.

## Premise
In the fictional story, Stephen travels to China with an invented mysterious boyfriend and appears to vanish. David and Paul (first-name fictional characters), whose espionage credentials are almost nonexistent, attempt a catastrophically ill-informed rescue. The fictional resolutions include a bizarre mountain cult, outlandish detention bureaucracy, and an absurd black-market/body-assembly puzzle.

## Privacy and fictionalisation

Only Stephen Martin's real-world full name is permitted. Use fictional first-name character labels for David and Paul, no additional real identities, actual workplace or contact details, or private third-party relationship/family background. Events and allegations are fictional and satirical. The public repository must contain no private biographical details, and a real name plus stylised likeness is not a guarantee of anonymity.

## Scale and success
Keep 2–3 meaningful mini-games or set-piece puzzles, limited locations, simple interactions and three distinct short finales through one shared adventure. A tight, genuinely funny complete game is the aim. No MMO, sprawling world, long playthrough, live-service backend or elaborate narrative branches. All expansions require explicit sign-off.
